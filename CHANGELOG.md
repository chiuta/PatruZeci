## 3.2.1 — 2026-11-15

- New: hyper-detailed fractal textures (fff-texturi 1.1.2) — the background and large surfaces (cards, panels, headers, buttons; tabs and dialogs that are hidden at load, once they open) get a locally generated material (WebGL2, no network, no images) that tiles seamlessly; the app keeps its own colours. Turn it off in the "Transparency" dialog or with `?notex=1`.

## 3.2.0 — 2026-11-14

- New: the whole interface in 8 languages (English, Română, Français, Italiano, Español, Português, Deutsch, Magyar): tabs, filters, categories, sections, risk levels, safety notes, help texts, sources. Language is picked from the browser or from the selector in the header and remembered.
- The texts of the uses (147 guide entries, 2018 list entries, product range, “what to avoid”) now exist in Romanian and English; the other six languages show them in English for now.
- Installable and offline: web manifest, service worker (stale-while-revalidate) and icons; strict Content-Security-Policy (no network requests) generated by `tools/build.py`, which also writes `app.json`.
- Phone header: language selector and a compact animations toggle; shorter tab labels so six tabs fit in every language.
- New screenshots (English) plus German, Spanish and Hungarian interface shots; READMEs updated in all 8 languages.

## 3.1.0 — 2026-11-14

- New: 25 extra uses, myths and warnings from recent web articles (section 🌐 “Din alte surse web” in the full list), paraphrased and risk-marked.
- Sources tab extended with 8 new references.

## 3.0.0 — 2026-11-14

- New: **Full list** tab with all 1993 uses from the official “2000+ uses” PDF, translated into Romanian, each with its own animation (131 scene variants chosen automatically), six official sections, function filters, search, infinite scroll and a detail sheet with prev/next navigation.
- 112 uses marked “not recommended” (brakes, seat belts, food contact, skin and animals, medical devices, vinyl records) and 245 marked “use caution”, with explanations.
- The old text-only “extended index” was removed (replaced by the full list).
